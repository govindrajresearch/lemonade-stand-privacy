Children's data. We do not track or monitor the behaviour of children (players under 18), do not show them targeted advertising, and do not process their data in ways likely to harm their well-being. Game decisions of players under 18 are not collected, unless the child joins the optional research study described below. Otherwise only anonymous app-usage counts are used to improve the game.

Optional research study for players under 18. Once approved by the KLS Gogte Institute of Technology ethics committee (the approval reference is shown in the app), players under 18 may be invited to an educational research study on how young people learn business decisions. They join only if a parent or guardian gives specific consent and the child agrees, on a separate screen. Only game decisions, the age group and the player ID are shared, never the name, photo, phone, email or address. The same storage and time limits apply (180 days in Firebase, then a private archive for up to 3 years), results are published only as summaries, and the parent or the child can stop at any time without any change to the game. This study is never a condition of using the app.
