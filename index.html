Privacy policy

Lemonade Stand business simulation game · Effective 12 November 2026

Lemonade Stand is designed and published by Dr. Govindraj R. Mane, Faculty, Department of MBA, KLS Gogte Institute of Technology ("we", "us"). This policy explains what information the app handles and why. It is part of the app's Terms of the Agreement, which every player (or, for players under 18, their parent or guardian) accepts before playing.

1. Information you choose to give us

You can create a profile with your name, date of birth, gender, degree, college, university, postal address (address, city, state, PIN or ZIP code, country), email address and mobile number. A profile is needed before you start playing.

When you save a profile, the app tells you that your details are collected, and a copy is sent to a Google Sheet that we own and control, so we can contact you about training programmes and workshops.

Age. Lemonade Stand is for players aged 8 and over. The app does not accept a profile from anyone younger than 8.

Players under 18. If the date of birth shows a player is under 18, a parent or legal guardian must give their name, relationship, mobile number and email address and tick a consent declaration before the profile is saved or shared. We use the parent's or guardian's contact details only to confirm consent and to respond to their requests, and the record is marked as a minor's.

2. How we use it

  • To tell you about training programmes, workshops and updates to the game.
  • To understand, in summary form, which colleges and courses use the game.

We do not sell your information, and we do not share it with anyone else except where the law requires it.

3. Where it is stored and for how long

Shared profiles are stored in Google Sheets in our Google account, protected by Google's security. We keep a profile until you ask us to delete it, and in any case for no longer than three years after your last update.

4. Your choices and rights

  • See or correct your details at any time on the app's My profile page. Corrections update our copy.
  • Withdraw consent or have your details deleted (a parent or guardian may do this for a player under 18) by emailing grmanegames@gmail.com from the email address in your profile. We delete our copy within 30 days and confirm by email.
  • You can also email us to get a copy of your information or to raise a concern.

5. Game progress, profile photo and shared cards

Your game progress, badges and settings are stored only on your phone. Uninstalling the app removes them.

Profile photo. If you add a photo, it is kept only on your phone. It is never sent to us. It appears only on a result card that you choose to share, and never for players under 13.

Share my profit. When you share a result card, the app hands a picture to the app you choose (such as WhatsApp). The card shows your name, player ID, the date and time, and your game result. Players under 13 show only their first name and an avatar.

6. Leaderboards (only if you join)

Leaderboards are off until you turn them on. If you join, the app signs in anonymously to Google Firebase (no email or password) and sends your first name and last initial, your player ID, your college, state and country, and your game scores (this week's profit, best day and number of badges). Other players can see these on the leaderboards. Players under 13 show only their first name, and no college. Your phone number, email, address and photo are never sent. You can leave the leaderboards at any time in Settings; to have your scores removed, email us.

7. Business report, data export and game decisions for research

Decision log. The app keeps a record of each game day (your prices, supplies, advertising and results) on your phone to show your Business Report. You can export it as a CSV file; it goes only where you choose to send it.

Game decisions for research (players 18 and over; a condition of using the app). For players aged 18 or over, each game day's decisions and results are sent to Google Firebase (stored in Mumbai, India) with the player ID, the date and time and an optional class code, but not the name, phone, email or address. Dr. Govindraj R. Mane uses them for research and teaching in business studies and to improve the game, reported only in summary or anonymised form. Records older than 180 days are moved to a private Google Sheets archive in our Google account and deleted from Firebase; the archive is kept for no longer than three years. Consent is given by accepting the Terms of the Agreement and can be withdrawn in Settings (the game then stops until it is accepted again); email us to delete records already shared. Game decisions of players under 18 are collected only if they join the optional, ethics-approved research study, with specific consent from a parent or guardian and the child's agreement; either can stop at any time, and it never affects the game.

8. App usage and crash reports

The app uses Google Firebase Analytics to count how the game is used (for example, which screens are opened and how many days are played), and Firebase Crashlytics to receive a report if the app crashes. These do not collect your advertising ID or your profile details, and we do not use them for advertising. See Firebase privacy information.

9. Advertising

The free version shows ads from Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, Google may collect your device's advertising ID, approximate location from your IP address, and information about ad interactions. Where required, the app asks for your consent before showing personalised ads. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's Google settings. See how Google uses information from apps.

10. Purchases

The ad-free subscription is processed entirely by Google Play. We do not receive your payment card details; we only learn whether a subscription is active.

11. Changes

If we change this policy, we will update the date above and, for significant changes, tell you in the app.
